# Keja AI Strategy

> PDF edition: `docs/pdf/keja-strategy.pdf`. Kenya-first, Africa-next. State: September 2026.

## 1. Executive Summary

**Thesis**: property is Kenya's largest store of household wealth, its information environment is pre-digital, and portals solved advertising while nobody solved verification-analysis-transaction as one explainable layer. Keja AI is that layer. Three phases: prove the trust product with consumers and agents on self-growing inventory; monetise professionals and institutions; become transactional infrastructure earning fees where trust converts into completed deals. Each phase has explicit gates — no phase begins until its predecessor's metrics clear. The engine is unusually cheap to run: static edge deployment, automated inventory growth, no servers to babysit.

## 2. Vision, Mission, Principles

**Vision**: a continent where any property can be trusted from a phone. **Mission**: verify every property, explain every number, guide every transaction in African real estate.

- **Evidence over promises** — drillable scores, declared sample sizes, public claims register.
- **Small surface, deep integrity** — features ship only when they strengthen the trust spine.
- **Capital efficiency as strategy** — runway measured in years, not months.
- **Local by default** — trilingual, offline-capable, shilling-denominated, WhatsApp-native.

## 3. Problem and Opportunity

| Structural failure | Cost | Keja's wedge |
|---|---|---|
| Title/identity opacity | Manual, expert-gated due diligence; endemic fraud | Property Passport: one identity document |
| Price/yield opacity | Blind negotiation | Valuation bands, Investment Scores, live yields with sample sizes |
| Distance risk (diaspora) | Remittances meet zero inspection ability | Remote verification, Deal Analyst, timezone-aware service |

Market layers: consumers supply volume and habit; professionals supply recurring revenue; institutions supply contracts and data economics. Kenya is the beachhead (digital payments rails, active diaspora, upgradeable mortgage market, land-records digitisation momentum). Regional expansion reuses the whole engine — only data and partners change.

## 4. Competitive Landscape and Moat

| Class | Examples | Strength | Structural gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Portals | BuyRentKenya, Jiji, PigiaMe | Traffic, breadth | Verification slows their flywheel, cannibalises ads |
| Agency/data houses | Hass, Knight Frank | Institutional trust | Bespoke pricing; no consumer self-serve trust |
| Government rails | Lands records, eCitizen | Authoritative data | Records, not products |
| Informal channel | Brokers, family | Reach | Zero tooling, zero accountability |

Defensible, compounding assets: verification methodology + evidence trail, Auto-Pilot inventory pipeline, explainable-scoring engine partners can audit, claims-register discipline as brand, near-zero distribution cost. Where portals must keep volume frictionless, Keja's revenue depends on listing quality.

## 5. Business Model

| Stream | Payer | Model | Phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verified listing fees | Owners, agents, developers | Per-listing + volume tiers | 1-2 |
| Pro workspace SaaS | Agents, valuers, managers | Monthly subscription | 2 |
| Finance referrals | Banks, KMRC lenders | Commission on qualified introductions | 2-3 |
| Transaction services | Buyers and sellers | Fee on milestone-managed deals (partner-operated) | 3 |
| Data licensing | Banks, insurers, researchers | Subscription to anonymised intelligence | 3 |
| Verification API | Lenders, REITs, insurers | Per-call scoring for underwriting | 3 |

Disciplines: consumer never pays for trust basics (they are the growth engine); anything touching client money runs through licensed partners; tokenization stays trial-mode with fictional assets until CMA engagement concludes.

## 6. Go-To-Market Phases

| Phase | Thesis | Gate to advance |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Prove (0-6m) | Consumers + early agents adopt the trust layer | 3,000 installs; 100 agents; 20% MoM verified-view growth |
| 2 — Monetise (6-15m) | Pro subs, listing fees, first bank referral pilots | 150 paying pros; referral revenue; <5% monthly churn |
| 3 — Institutionalise (15-24m) | Verification API + data licensing embed Keja in underwriting | Two institutional contracts; transaction revenue live |

## 7. Technology as Strategy

- **Zero marginal cost of distribution** — statically exported PWA installs on any Android/iOS device, works offline.
- **Self-growing inventory** — Auto-Pilot ingests every six hours; supply scales with compute, not headcount.
- **Honesty as architecture** — claims register, sample-size declarations, drillable scores: the precondition for institutional revenue.

## 9. Risk Register

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Portal copies verification claims | High | They can copy words, not evidence trails; audit-ability becomes the comparison test |
| Verification partnerships stall | High | Graceful degradation (declared status, never fake certainty); professional panel fills gap |
| Consumer monetisation lags | Medium | Cost structure tolerates long runway; pro revenue is independent |
| Tokenization reclassification | Medium | Trial mode with fictional assets until CMA concludes; module isolation |
| Key-person concentration | Medium | Dossier, CI gates, this document reduce bus factor |
| Auto-Pilot data drift | Medium | Quality gate + pending-review queue already in the pipeline |
