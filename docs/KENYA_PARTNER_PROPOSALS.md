# Kenya Partner Proposals — Twenty Targets

> PDF edition: `docs/pdf/keja-kenya-partner-proposals.md` source of truth: `docs/pdf/keja-kenya-partner-proposals.pdf`. Each profile: who they are / why Keja fits / the proposal / the ask.

## 1. Partnership Philosophy and Tier Model

Trust infrastructure is a network good — valuable to each participant in proportion to how many others participate. We prioritise partnerships that compound, and sequence them so early partners get founder-level access and pricing for the credibility they lend.

| Tier | Meaning | Partner contributes | Keja contributes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Anchor | Co-branded flagship integration | Credibility, distribution, data or capital | Priority roadmap, co-branded trust surfaces, time-boxed category exclusivity |
| 2 — Revenue | Referral and licensing economics | Qualified flow or underwriting volume | Verified pipeline, eligibility tooling, per-deal reporting |
| 3 — Ecosystem | Data and standards collaboration | Authoritative data, standards, research reach | Anonymised intelligence, joint publications, integration |

**Outreach sequence**: one-page written proposal → warm introduction → 30-minute working session (not a pitch) → scoped 90-day pilot with named success metrics → commercial terms. We never propose what the engineering cannot deliver.

## 2. Banking and Mortgage Partners

**KCB Bank Kenya** — largest mortgage book, digitally aggressive, diaspora franchise. *Fit*: origination cost + collateral uncertainty. *Proposal*: 90-day pilot — KCB products surfaced in Keja Finance for qualifying users; verified property files to loan officers; co-branded badge post-pilot. *Ask*: pilot sponsor in origination + two working sessions.

**Equity Bank Kenya** — retail giant, remittance rails, housing agenda. *Fit*: remittance customers are Keja's diaspora personas. *Proposal*: "verify before you send" step integrated into the remittance journey + mortgage referrals. *Ask*: joint session with diaspora banking + partnerships.

**NCBA Bank Kenya** — digitally led, asset-finance DNA. *Fit*: underwriting against verified collateral. *Proposal*: Keja verification embedded in property-backed lending workflows, per-file licensing for the pilot. *Ask*: sandbox agreement + 90-day success metrics.

**Co-operative Bank** — the SACCO-movement bank. *Fit*: co-op housing finance is collateral-averse for good reason. *Proposal*: members' verification programme — Passports for co-op-funded developments, committee dashboards, co-branded education. *Ask*: pilot with two housing co-operatives.

**Stanbic Bank Kenya** — Standard Bank group franchise. *Fit*: clients buy property as an asset class; regional footprint. *Proposal*: private-client offering — investor dashboards and Deal Analyst for wealth clients, anonymised intelligence for the research desk. *Ask*: wealth + partnerships session, pilot cohort.

**HF Group** — specialist housing finance, developer lending, affordable-housing mission. *Fit*: every part of the lifecycle map is relevant. *Proposal*: full-lifecycle anchor — verified developer profiles, HF-branded eligibility flow, Keja Manage onboarding. *Ask*: anchor-partner conversation with category exclusivity.

## 3. Mortgage Liquidity and Payments

**KMRC** — the refinancing facility for affordable mortgages. *Fit*: standardised, better-documented originations are exactly its mandate. *Proposal*: Keja-verified pre-screened applicant-plus-property files routed to participating lenders, aligned to KMRC criteria. *Ask*: alignment session + approval to route pilot files to two lenders.

**Safaricom (M-Pesa)** — the rails Kenyan money moves on. *Fit*: every Keja transaction surface runs better on M-Pesa; property is a large unmoved commerce category. *Proposal*: native M-Pesa integration with a co-marketed "verified property payments" story. *Ask*: developer-tier API agreement + joint go-to-market conversation.

## 4. Data, Standards and Government

**KNBS** — the national statistical authority. *Fit*: Keja wants authoritative anchors; KNBS methodology wants ground-truth observations. *Proposal*: data-collaboration memorandum — anonymised observations in, cited methodology out. *Ask*: exploratory scoping session.

**Ministry of Lands, Public Works, Housing and Urban Development** — custodian of records and digitisation. *Fit*: Keja is the consumer-facing layer digitisation needs; alignment future-proofs the Trust Score. *Proposal*: alignment framework recognising Keja's methodology as a citizen-facing complement with declared provenance and graceful degradation. *Ask*: stakeholder engagement on provenance standards.

**Institution of Surveyors of Kenya (ISK)** — the professional body behind due diligence. *Fit*: the evidence chain needs licensed valuers; members need digital demand. *Proposal*: professional-panel programme with member rates and co-developed digital evidence standards. *Ask*: standards session + pilot panel of member firms.

## 5. Agencies, Data Houses and Portals

**Hass Consult** — Kenya's reference index and research brand. *Proposal*: data-and-brand alliance — co-branded quarterly intelligence combining index methodology with live-inventory analytics. *Ask*: research collaboration session.

**Knight Frank Kenya** — prime and institutional mandates. *Proposal*: prime-listing verification — Knight Frank listings carry Keja Passports; institutional portal surfaces their research to qualified investors. *Ask*: pilot on one prime portfolio.

**Pam Golding Properties Kenya** — premium brand, diaspora recognition. *Proposal*: diaspora-facing alliance — co-branded remote verification, joint webinars, Deal Analyst referrals. *Ask*: joint diaspora-campaign pilot.

**BuyRentKenya** — the leading portal. *Proposal*: verification-layer partnership — premium listings opt into Keja verification with Trust Score badge; revenue shared per verification. *Ask*: commercial conversation scoped to an opt-in badge pilot.

## 6. Developer Partners

**Superior Homes Kenya** — gated-community pioneer, delivered-promise brand. *Proposal*: anchor verification case study — portfolio profiled with Development Scores and delivery-history evidence. *Ask*: anchor-developer agreement on one flagship community.

**Optiven Limited** — value-added land specialist, investor-first. *Proposal*: investor-verification programme — Passports on projects, Investment Score integrations for advisors, joint education content. *Ask*: pilot on two active projects.

**Karibu Homes** — affordable-housing production developer. *Proposal*: buyer-journey partnership — every unit ships with a Passport, lender pre-matching, co-branded buyer portal. *Ask*: pilot on next sales release.

## 7. Insurance and Community

**Britam Kenya** — insurance and asset management. *Proposal*: embedded-insurance programme — Britam home cover at the point of verified trust, risk-scored pricing informed by verification depth. *Ask*: product-partnership session with general insurance and digital teams.

**Kenya Diaspora Alliance** — the umbrella trust network for home-bound money. *Proposal*: community programme — chapter webinars, member access with prioritised verification slots, joint annual trust report. *Ask*: programme agreement + first chapter webinar within 60 days.

## 8. Outreach Sequence and Governance

| Wave | Partners | Timing | Success metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — credibility | KNBS, ISK, Ministry, KMRC, Diaspora Alliance | Weeks 1-6 | Two memoranda/programmes in motion |
| 2 — anchors | HF Group, KCB, Safaricom, Superior Homes, Hass | Weeks 4-12 | One signed anchor pilot |
| 3 — volume | Equity, NCBA, Co-op, Stanbic, agencies, developers, Britam, BuyRentKenya | Weeks 10-24 | Three active pilots; one converting to commercial terms |

Governance: one partnerships ledger (who/why/what/ask per conversation), weekly movement review, and a hard rule — no partnership promise may exceed what the claims register already declares live. Pilots are 90 days with pre-agreed metrics and a default-to-sunset clause. Signed partners are announced as shipped code (badges, passports, co-branded surfaces), not press releases.
