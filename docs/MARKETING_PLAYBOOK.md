# Keja AI Marketing Playbook

> PDF edition: `docs/pdf/keja-marketing-playbook.pdf`. Companion to `STRATEGY.md` and `KENYA_PARTNER_PROPOSALS.md`.

## 1. Market Snapshot

Kenya's property market is large, urbanising and chronically opaque. Buying or renting means trusting a broker met once, a title not readily verifiable, and a price negotiated without data. The diaspora layer multiplies every verification problem — remittances are among Kenya's top FX inflows and real estate is a top intended use.

**Competitive set**: listing portals (BuyRentKenya, Jiji, PigiaMe) monetise attention and leave trust to the buyer; agency/data houses (Hass Consult, Knight Frank) serve institutions at institutional prices; the informal channel (brokers, family) moves most volume with zero tooling. **Nobody owns the trust layer across all three. Keja attacks that gap: not more listings — listings you can act on.**

| Audience truth | Marketing consequence |
|---|---|
| Trust is the #1 unmet need | Lead with verification and evidence, never listing counts |
| Mobile-first, mid-range Android, data-sensitive | Advertise the install + offline story as features |
| WhatsApp is the default rail | Shareable Property Passports beat email funnels |
| Diaspora: money + anxiety in equal measure | Remote verification, timezone-aware service |
| Swahili/English coexist; French reaches region | Trilingual product is a differentiator to say out loud |

## 2. Positioning

**For every stakeholder in Kenyan real estate tired of guessing, Keja AI is the trust infrastructure that verifies properties, scores them transparently and guides the money — because a home is too expensive to buy on faith.**

- Buyers/tenants: explainable Trust Score + Property Passport on every listing.
- Owners/landlords: verified listings, rent collection, arrears engine.
- Investors/diaspora: yields, deal screening, on-device documents.
- Professionals: a verification and data layer clients can audit.

Brand line: *Discover. Verify. Analyse. Finance. Invest. Transact. Manage.* Consumer tagline: **"See the truth before you buy."**

Tone: calm, evidence-led, quietly confident — the anti-hype property brand. No "revolutionary", no urgency countdowns, no promised returns.

## 4. Message Pillars

1. **Proof** — "Every property, verified." Drillable scores, evidence panels, 90-day freshness.
2. **Clarity** — "Numbers you can interrogate." Yields with sample sizes, CBK 33% eligibility, Proceed/Negotiate/Investigate/High-Risk recommendations.
3. **Reach** — "Kenya's property app, in your pocket." Installable, offline-capable, trilingual.

| Convert | 5-8 | Installs (PWA campaign, WhatsApp lists, radio, Deal Analyst demo days) | PWA installs / weekly actives |
| Compound | 9-12 | Loops and partners (alerts, referrals, 2 bank/developer pilots, PR) | Weekly returning users |

## 7. Growth Loops

- **Passport loop**: share cards built for WhatsApp; every share is the product marketing itself.
- **Alert loop**: Auto-Pilot keeps adding inventory; saved-search alerts bring users back.
- **Professional loop**: agents get better-qualified enquiries from verified stock → more supply → better buyer experience.

Retention follows the honesty principle: onboarding shows the claims register; install prompt dismisses for 14 days; broadcasts carry one useful thing, never hype.
